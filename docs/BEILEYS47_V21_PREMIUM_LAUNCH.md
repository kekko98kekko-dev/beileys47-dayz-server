## Fair survival/PvP rules (copy-ready, EN)

1. PvP is allowed in the survival world, not only in hotspots. Do not promise safe zones unless actually configured.
2. No cheating, duplication exploits, deliberate server-crash abuse, hateful harassment or doxxing.
3. Building and raiding follow native DayZ mechanics. There are no scripted raid windows; do not advertise them.
4. Public announcement content is promotional, not a guaranteed event start or automatic payout.
5. Cases, colored keys and special items are scarce and probabilistic. Finding a key is not proof a shipping-container lock works on Livonia.
6. No scheduled wipes is a policy; the world cannot be promised immune to provider restores or game updates.
7. Admin decisions and event prizes require evidence and a recorded manual review; no pay-to-win.
8. Report bugs privately with time, approximate coordinates and sanitized screenshots/videos. Avoid posting player data publicly.

## New-player experience

Within 15 minutes players should learn: how to get food and water, bandage injuries, how stamina works in V20, where hotspots are and where to go for supplies. They spawn according to the installed spawn configuration, **not** necessarily next to an arena. Do not falsely advertise a safe starter hub, free guns on demand or admin teleport.

**Quick tips for a public page or Discord onboarding:**

- **First 5 min:** gather food/water and a bandage, check clothing condition.
- **Next 5 min:** find a knife or cutting tool, learn to repair clothing and avoid damaged shoes.
- **Next 5 min:** choose an approach: quiet survival, Dolnik supplies, or high-risk ZONA 47 PvP.
- **Always:** a full sprint bar does not remove fall damage, infected threats, weapon degradation or starvation.

## Balancing goals — not invented live results

- Do not fill every site with military weapons. Keep logistics, survival food and medical value distinct.
- Differentiate risk: Dolnik supplies (basic to intermediate), Radunin PvP (military encounters), bunker (rare contested finds), distributed vanilla convoys/crashes.
- Test 10-player concurrency before adding more infected/structures; keep escape routes wide enough for PS4 performance.
- Keep vehicles repairable but not instantly replacing stolen persistent trucks; preserve genuine survival loops.
- Check repair contents of **newly spawned** vehicles, not persisted ones, including gasoline identity, attachment inventories and available slots.
- Avoid increasing all loot nominal values simultaneously: central economy is stateful and may take time to stabilize.
